CRMExtender

HomePrivacy policySource code

CRMExtender is a relationship intelligence tool and mail client that runs on its operator's own computer. It mirrors the operator's Gmail mailboxes, Google Contacts and Google Calendar into a local database, groups messages into conversations, links them to the people and companies involved, and lets the operator read, reply, label, snooze and track mail from one interface across every account.

The software is open source and is published on GitHub. This site exists to identify the application to Google for the OAuth consent process and to carry its privacy policy.

What the application accesses

  • Gmail: read messages and labels, change labels and read state, and send mail on the operator's behalf.
  • Google Contacts: read contact names, email addresses and groups.
  • Google Calendar: read events.
